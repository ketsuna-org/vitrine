In short

What is stored where, honestly.

  • If you only use the app with your own bot and local features: bot configuration and your bot token stay on your device (token in the system secure storage). The app still sends usage analytics and crash reports to third parties (see “Analytics and crash reporting”) and shows ads unless you subscribe.
  • If you sign in with Discord, use cloud hosting, the Copilot (AI) or the web console: your account, your bots’ configuration and, when you save it to the cloud, your bot token are stored on our servers (the token is encrypted at rest). Earlier versions of this policy said tokens were never stored in a database. That was not accurate for cloud features and is corrected here.
  • AI Copilot messages are sent to a third-party AI provider (DeepSeek).
  • There is currently no self-service “delete my account” or “export my data” button. You exercise your rights by email (see “Your rights”).
  • We do not sell your data. Discord data is used only to run the service you asked for, and is not sold or licensed to anyone.

Analytics and crash reporting

Used inside the apps; there is no opt-out setting yet.

The apps use Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Performance Monitoring (Android, iOS and web builds; not the desktop builds) and PostHog (all platforms, EU-hosted at eu.i.posthog.com). They collect events (screens, app open, creating a bot or a command, subscription steps), device type, operating system, language, performance traces, crash stack traces, IP address and installation identifiers.

When you are signed in, your Discord user ID is sent as is (not hashed) to Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and PostHog, together with properties such as platform, premium tier, number of bots (bucketed) and language.

For about 20 % of installations, PostHog also records session replays of the app screen. At the moment text and images displayed on screen are not masked, so a replay can show what you see in the app, including bot names and command contents. There is currently no setting in the app to switch analytics off. You can object to this processing by writing to contact@bot-creator.fr. Retention is set by the providers (Crashlytics crash reports are kept for up to 90 days); we do not control a shorter period.

Discord data and bot tokens

How we handle data that comes from Discord.

  • We use Discord data (your identity, your applications and their team, and the events your bots receive) only to provide the features you use. We do not sell it, license it to data brokers, or use it to build advertising profiles. The Discord user ID is, however, sent to the analytics providers listed above.
  • Bots run on your device, on our runners or on a runner you host. A bot’s gateway intents are those you enable in the bot configuration and the Discord developer portal (for example Guild Members, Presences or Message Content). Runners process events in memory to execute your commands. Our logs and statistics do not record message content; data that your own commands save into variables or into a database you connect is stored as you configured it.
  • If you run a bot in a Discord server, you are responsible for informing and obtaining rights from the members of that server. For the data your bots save through Bot Creator, you are the controller and we act as your processor, storing it on your instruction.
  • Bot tokens are credentials governed by Discord’s Developer Terms of Service and Developer Policy. You can revoke a token at any time in the Discord developer portal; Bot Creator will then be unable to connect your bot.
  • On request we delete the Discord data associated with your account (see “Data deletion”). Bot Creator is an independent service and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Discord.

Web console and dashboards

Cookies, storage and who is responsible.

Cookies. The console and the dashboards set one strictly necessary session cookie each: bcm_creator_app (console, app.bot-creator.fr) and bcm_cms_dash (member sign-in on a dashboard). They contain your session tokens, are signed but not encrypted, HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, Secure, bound to the host, and expire with the session. The console also stores two “tutorial already seen” flags and a panel width in your browser’s localStorage. We set no advertising or tracking cookie there.

Third-party content. The console and dashboards load fonts from Google Fonts and, on the console, a script from jsDelivr (GSAP); Discord CDN avatars are displayed; dashboard owners can embed YouTube or Vimeo videos. Each of these receives your IP address when displayed.

Dashboards hosted for bot owners. A dashboard at dash-<id>.bot-creator.fr or on a custom domain is operated by the owner of the bot. The owner decides its content and is responsible towards its visitors; Bot Creator hosts it and processes sign-in data for the owner. Default “Terms” and “Privacy” pages are generated for each dashboard as placeholders that the owner must replace with their own.

Payments

Stores, Stripe and Ko-fi.

App stores (Apple, Google, Steam) process payments for subscriptions bought there; we receive transaction identifiers and status notifications. Stripe processes web subscriptions (Checkout is hosted by Stripe; we never see your card number) and is the payment provider of creators who sell through Stripe Connect. Ko-fi notifies us of donations or purchases made there. We keep identifiers and status, not card data.

Data deletion

Request deletion of your Bot Creator account and data.

How to request deletion

There is no self-service deletion yet. Send an email to contact@bot-creator.fr with the subject “Data deletion request”, from the address linked to your account or mentioning your Discord user ID so that we can identify you. We reply without undue delay and, in any case, within one month.

What is deleted

  • Your account and Discord / email identity, OAuth tokens and sessions.
  • Your bots, their configuration, variables, versions, statistics, and the stored bot tokens and credentials (also from object storage and Redis).
  • Your activity log and its stored payloads.
  • Your AI Copilot conversations, requests and results, and your contributions to the shared command library (an entry shared with another contributor stays for them).
  • Purchase links, collaborator links and team messages tied to your account.

What may remain

  • Aggregated AI usage and cost counters, which are kept without the content of your conversations.
  • Purchase and invoice records that we must keep for accounting and tax purposes.
  • Administrative audit entries and technical logs held by our infrastructure providers, until they expire. [TO COMPLETE: confirm audit-log and backup retention]
  • Data held by third parties under their own policies: Google (Firebase, AdMob), PostHog, Stripe, Apple, Google Play, Discord. Write to them directly to exercise your rights on data they hold as independent controllers.
  • Data stored on your own device (including your token in secure storage) and in your own Google Drive, which you delete yourself.

Your rights

GDPR and other privacy laws.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to processing and port your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. There is no self-service export yet: on request we send you your data in a structured, commonly used format, within one month. Write to contact@bot-creator.fr. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).
