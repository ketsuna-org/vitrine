The referral is a give-and-take: you invite a friend with your personal code, and both of you get AI credits. Every bonus is generated by the platform. Nobody gives credits to anybody else.

## Your personal code

Every account has one personal code, written like `ABCD-EFGH`. Open **Settings > Referral** in the mobile app or in the Studio to see it, copy it or share a link that already contains it (`?ref=ABCD-EFGH`). The code does not change.

## Enter a friend's code

You can enter a code in two places:

- **When you sign in with Discord.** Open "I have an invitation code" on the sign-in screen and type the code, or open the invitation link and it is filled in for you. The code is applied once you are signed in. If it is refused, you are signed in anyway and a short message tells you why.
- **In Settings > Referral**, up to 7 days after your account was created.

You can enter one code, once. You cannot use your own code.

## What each of you gets

| Who | When | Bonus |
|-----|------|-------|
| The friend who enters the code | Right away | 50 AI credits |
| You, the person who invited | For each friend who becomes qualified | 50 AI credits |
| You, at 3 qualified friends | Once | 200 more AI credits |
| You, at 10 qualified friends | Once | 7 days of Premium |

A friend is **qualified** when their Discord account is more than 30 days old and one of their bots has run for at least 30 minutes. Until then the referral shows as pending and you receive nothing for it.

## Limits

- Up to 20 friends per year count for one person.
- The platform has a monthly budget for referral bonuses. When it is used up, new codes are refused until the next month.
- A code can only be entered during the first 7 days of an account.
- If a referral is cancelled, for example because of abuse, the bonuses already paid stay with their owner. A cancelled referral pays nothing more.

## Privacy

You only see how many friends are pending and qualified, and how many credits you earned. You never see who they are. Your friend does not see anything about you either.

## Where it is available

The referral is in the mobile app (Android and iOS) and in the Studio. It is not in the desktop app.

AI credits are used by the [AI Copilot](/docs/ai-copilot/).
